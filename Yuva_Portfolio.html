Featured project

master's thesis · IMTEK × Hahn-Schickard

Performance Evaluation of a LAM-based Lateral Flow Assay Integrated with a Centrifugal Microfluidic Platform for Tuberculosis Diagnosis

Sputum-based TB testing is slow and hard to deploy outside a lab. This project targeted the ULAM biomarker as a non-invasive alternative — integrating a lateral flow immunoassay directly onto a centrifugal microfluidic disk, the first such integration, as a step toward point-of-care tuberculosis diagnosis without a sputum sample.

  • Optimized assay sensitivity to a limit of detection between 1 and 5 ng/mL
  • Diagnosed root causes of microfluidic flow failures and redesigned the disk geometry and protocol around them
  • Validated with fluorescence microscopy and quantitative ImageJ analysis
